    How the contest works

    1 · Create

    Make your combo in the Overlay Builder, sign in (Discord or Commander name + PIN), then press Submit to Contest. Add a caption if you like. No wallet needed.

    2 · React

    Each reaction (👍 ❤️ 🔥 😂 😮) is 1 point. You can give an entry up to 5, one of each. Score is everyone's reactions added up.

    3 · Ant-Thony judges

    When the clock hits zero, voting closes and Ant-Thony reviews every entry, picks his finalists and posts his verdict in Discord.

    4 · Winners

    The top entries win. Prizes are $REBEL Points, NFTs, merch or bragging rights, and change by round.

    5 · Share

    Use Share on a card to post it on X with a link people can vote from.

    6 · Fair play

    Repeat reactions are blocked and we watch for abuse. See the Rules and Moderation.

    $REBEL Points are internal Rebel Ants ecosystem loyalty points. They are not cryptocurrency, not cash, not an investment, not transferable, and not redeemable for cash.
